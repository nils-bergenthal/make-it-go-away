# Contributing

Thank you for helping. The skill should stay small and plain. Fewer words are better.

## Good contributions

- A real case where the skill missed something, with names and numbers replaced.
- A short invented example conversation for another kind of work.
- Wording for another language. Do not translate word for word. Think how a person says it at the kitchen table.
- A fix for a word or question that a layperson did not understand.

## Rules

- Use your own words. No text from courses, books, standards or other skills.
- No product names, brand names or logos of other companies in the skill files.
- No web addresses, no scripts, no encoded text in `skills/`.
- Questions stay plain: short sentences, one question at a time, a choice or examples.
- Do not add legal texts. The skill points to the rules that apply to the user and says it is not legal advice.
- If you share test results, give the raw numbers, including the bad ones, and say how you tested.
