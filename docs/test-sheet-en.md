# Test sheet: trying make-it-go-away

Thank you for helping. It takes about 20 minutes.

## Before
- Pick a task from your daily work that keeps coming back and annoys you.
- Replace names and numbers. Do not enter real customer or patient data.

## Steps
1. Start a new conversation and say: `Make it go away: [your problem, described briefly]`
2. Answer the questions. Say "result now" when you have had enough.

## Questions afterwards
1. Did it ask something you would not have thought of on your own? What?
2. Was everything easy to understand? Which word or question was unclear?
3. Would you start building with the result and hand it on? What was missing?
4. Would you recommend it to a colleague? Why, or why not?
5. Was anything too long, too short or too much?

Where to send your answers: use the feedback form under "Issues" on the GitHub page (a free account is needed). It has the same questions. Or write your answers down and give them to whoever shared the skill with you.
