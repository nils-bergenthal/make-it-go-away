# Example: thank-you mails in a bike shop (an honest "leave it")

This example is invented. All names, firms and numbers are made up. It shows what happens when a task is too small to be worth planning.

Tobias runs a small bike shop. He talks to Claude with the skill switched on.

## The conversation

**Tobias:** Make it go away: writing a personal thank-you mail to everyone who buys a bike.

**Claude:**

```
This usually takes 10 to 20 minutes. After a few questions I will show you a first draft.
You can say "result now" at any time. This chat runs through the company behind this AI,
so leave out confidential things. Replace names and numbers before you paste anything.
Then show me the last real case.
```

**Tobias:** Last week a woman bought a city bike. I wrote: thanks, here is your invoice, come back for the first check.

**Claude:**

```
🔹 **Result at the end**

What should be there when this is done?

  a) A ready mail with the name and the bike in it
  b) A ready mail that I read and send
  c) Something else, describe it briefly
```

**Tobias:** b

**Claude:**

```
🔹 **How often**

How often does this happen, roughly?

  a) About 5 times a month
  b) About 50 times a month
  c) About 500 times a month
```

**Tobias:** About 10.

**Claude:**

```
🔹 **Build or not**

At 10 a month, I would not automate this. Planning on takes about 10 to 20 minutes
and around 20 messages. That costs more than it brings when a task saves little time.
A template with two gaps, for name and bike, takes you one minute per mail. What do you want?

  a) Make the manual way lighter, with a template
  b) Plan on anyway, and I write why in one sentence
  c) Something else, describe it briefly

**My suggestion:** a)
```

**Tobias:** a. You are right.

## The result in short form

```
Self-check
Contradictions: none found.
Assumptions: none.
Gaps: none that change the answer.
```

**Verdict: do not build.**

- **Reason:** About 10 mails a month are too few. A template does the job.
- **For the boss, in one sentence:** No, this is not worth planning, because a template saves nearly the same time.
- **What to do instead:** Write one mail with gaps for the name and the bike. Keep it in a note on your computer and fill it in for each sale.
- **What would have to change for a later yes:** Many more mails a month, or the mail should pull data from your shop program by itself. Then come back.

**Final recommendation**

Leave it. Use the template.
